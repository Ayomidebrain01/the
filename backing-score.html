Good ideas don't always get backed.

An idea can be strong and still struggle to get the support it needs.

Sometimes the challenge isn't the idea itself. It's whether people understand it, see the value, believe in it or feel confident enough to support the decision.

The Backing Score gives you an outside perspective on some of the factors that can influence whether an important idea gets understood, believed in and backed.

Make it clear. Make it matter. Make it land.

The Backing Score sits within Project Brand's wider Make It Land point of view.

Because important ideas don't just need to be good. They need to make sense to the people you're asking to understand them, support them, approve them, invest in them or act on them.

The assessment helps identify where your idea is already strong and where there may be friction worth addressing.

What makes an idea easier to back?

The Backing Score looks at five things that shape whether people can understand an idea, believe in it, and feel ready to support it.

01

Clarity

Is the idea easy to understand?

02

Proposition

Is the value clear and compelling?

03

Belief

Is there enough substance to make people believe in it?

04

Relevance

Does it matter to the people whose support you need?

05

Confidence

Are there obvious reasons for people to hesitate, question or delay?
