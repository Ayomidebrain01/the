Our Story

ROOTED IN JAPAN. BUILT FOR THE WORLD.

Project Brand was born in Tokyo in 2020 — from a leap of faith and two decades of sports marketing experience that needed a new home.

Our founder Sarah spent years working across global brands, rights holders, and sporting bodies — from the Springboks to ASICS (three years at HQ in Tokyo) to adidas Running. Along the way she kept bumping into the same problem: sports brands either overpay for big agency overhead or underpay for freelancers who don't know the industry.

So she built something in between. A studio inspired by 改善 Kaizen — the Japanese philosophy of continuous improvement. Which basically means we're never done getting better. Sounds intense. It is. But it works.

KAIZEN Continuous improvement. Small steps, big gains.
What We Do

WHAT WE'RE GOOD AT.

Four areas. All powered by AI where it makes sense — and always backed by people who know sport inside out.

Strategy
We don't do strategy decks that collect dust. We build plans you can actually use — across marketing, content, sponsorship, sales, and go-to-market. We use AI to accelerate the research and sharpen the insights, so you get to the good stuff faster.
AI-powered research, briefs & creative
Digital
Your brand lives online whether you like it or not. We make sure it shows up well — compelling messaging, digital campaigns, websites that actually work, and SEO that earns attention. Built around what your audience cares about, not what looks good in a pitch.
Creative
Ideas are easy. Execution is everything. We handle brand development, campaign creative, photography, videography, and content production — pulling from a global network of talent we trust. If it needs to look good and land hard, that's us.
Management
Someone needs to keep the trains running. We handle project management, event coordination, timelines, budgets, and athlete concierge — so you can focus on the big picture while we take care of the details.
Our Work

SOME THINGS WE'VE DONE.

Rugby — URC
Commercial Partnership Programme

The challenge

A leading rugby competition needed to build a partnership activation programme that could demonstrate real value to commercial partners — and fast.

What we did

Developed the partnership strategy from scratch, created activation plans tied to measurable KPIs, and managed partner relationships across the full commercial calendar.

The result

A structured commercial programme that gave partners clarity, confidence, and a reason to stay. Helped shape how the organisation approached partnership value long-term.

Media — Habarii Media
Commercial Revenue Strategy

The challenge

A fast-growing sports media business needed commercial proposals that could land serious partnerships — the kind that move a business forward, not just keep the lights on.

What we did

Built out the commercial revenue decks and partnership proposals from the ground up. Positioned the business as a credible, high-value partner for brands looking to reach engaged sports audiences.

The result

Proposals valued at R2.5 million — and they landed. Multiple partnerships secured, with clients feeding back that the quality of the proposal itself was what got them over the line.

Sportswear — Adidas
Product Franchise Launch

The challenge

One of the world's biggest sportswear brands was launching their fastest running franchise — and needed global communications that could land across markets, retail, and digital simultaneously.

What we did

Led global communications strategy for the franchise launch. Built the campaign narrative, developed sell-in and sell-out toolkits, and ensured a seamless retail experience from global brief to local execution.

The result

A product launch that landed with consistency and impact across multiple markets — with the comms strategy doing the heavy lifting from day one.
