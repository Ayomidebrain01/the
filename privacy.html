Privacy

Updated 6 October 2026

This page explains what information Project Brand collects through theprojectbrand.com and The Backing Score, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Project Brand is a sports marketing studio based in London. For the information described on this page, you can reach us at sarah@theprojectbrand.com.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to send us, plus a small amount of technical information needed to run the site.

  • Contact form. If you write to us through the site, we receive your name, organisation, email address and message. That form is delivered by Formspree.
  • The Backing Score. If you take the assessment, we receive the answers you submit and any contact details you choose to include. Completed assessments are stored in a Google Sheet so we can read the result and follow up if you ask us to.
  • Email. If you email us directly, we keep that correspondence so we can reply.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider may record basic connection information, such as an IP address, in server logs. The site also stores a timestamp in your browser so it can refresh its cache. That timestamp is not linked to your name or email.

We do not sell personal information, and this site does not run advertising cookies.

How we use it

  • To reply to enquiries and talk about possible work.
  • To produce a Backing Score result and, if you leave contact details, to follow up about the idea.
  • To keep the website working and understand basic reliability issues.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the services that help us run the site and the assessment:

  • Formspree, which receives contact-form submissions.
  • Google Sheets, which stores completed Backing Score assessments.
  • The provider that hosts this website.

Those providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. They do so under their own privacy terms. We do not pass your information to anyone else for their own marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and assessment information for as long as we need it to respond, follow up, and keep a record of the conversation. You can ask us to delete it. We will, unless we need to keep a copy for a legal reason.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also object to how we use it. Email sarah@theprojectbrand.com.

If you are in the UK and you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Links to other sites

The site links to places such as LinkedIn. Those sites have their own privacy notices, and this page does not cover them.

Changes

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and the date at the top.
